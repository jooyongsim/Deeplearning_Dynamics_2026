Two equations used together — one for translation, one for rotation

Translation\(\displaystyle \sum\mathbf F = m\,\mathbf a_G\)
Rotation\(\displaystyle \sum M_G = I_G\,\alpha\)

Plane motion: three scalar equations

\[\sum F_x = m\,a_{Gx},\quad \sum F_y = m\,a_{Gy},\quad \sum M_G = I_G\,\alpha\]

Fixed-axis rotation about O

\[\sum M_O = I_O\,\alpha\]
Meriam & Kraige, Fig. 6/4 — forces on the FBD ≡ \(m\bar{\mathbf a}\) and \(\bar I\alpha\) on the kinetic diagram

Procedure : FBD ≡ kinetic diagram → kinematic link (e.g. \(a_G = R\alpha\)) → solve.

Starting point : almost every rigid-body kinetics problem starts from these two equations.
